Privacy Policy

Astralys · Last updated: 23 September 2026

Astralys is a mobile space-exploration game published by Astralys, 190 chemin de l’Homme Rouge, 13600 La Ciotat, France ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and your rights. Contact: contact@abundanceslots.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

  • Approximate location: if you allow it, your location is used on your device only, to calculate when your star is visible in your sky and for the home-screen widget (rounded to about 1 km). It is never sent to our servers.
  • Camera and motion sensors (sky-pointing feature): used live on your device, never recorded or sent.
  • Guest progress (when you play without an account).

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no selling or renting of your data.
  • No profiling for marketing.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase (database and authentication hosting) stores account and game data. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Google and Apple: optional sign-in, and payment processing through Google Play and the App Store. We never receive your card details.

5. Retention

Account and game data are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is erased immediately from our database (backups roll over within 30 days). Store purchase receipts are kept by Google and Apple under their own policies.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict its processing. Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or on this page. For any other request, write to contact@abundanceslots.com. You may also complain to your data-protection authority (in France: the CNIL, cnil.fr).

Legal bases: performance of the contract (account, game, purchases) and your consent (location, which you can withdraw in your phone settings).

7. Children

Astralys is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes.
